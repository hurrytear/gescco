The phrase "GPT 20x to 10x" refers to **ChatGPT Pro's $200/month tier** and its ChatGPT Work and Codex usage relative to Plus. It is not a model context-window change, an API rate limit, or a fixed message count for every account. Sources were checked on 2026-10-21; the current account dashboard and subscription terms determine your actual allowance.

## 01 / What was announced

On September 29, 2026, OpenAI's Thibault Sottiaux said in an [initial X post](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2104823812042940713) that reopening the Pro $200 tier to new subscribers would change the usage calculation. Measured in API-dollar equivalent, the new allowance would be about half the old plan's; the Pro tier would not regain a five-hour limit. His [follow-up](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2104951965184925941) set the new relative multipliers at Plus 1x, Pro $100 5x, and Pro $200 10x. Existing Pro $200 subscribers temporarily retain 20x and receive extra credits; that public post did not give an end date. A [public screenshot of a subscriber email](https://x.com/aipulseda1ly/status/2105043878373925312) says affected existing users retain the old allowance through October 29, with the change taking effect October 30. Eligibility and credit terms should be checked against your own notice.

As checked for this article, [OpenAI Docs pricing](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing) lists Pro tiers at $100, $200 and $500, says Work and Codex share subscription usage, and says Pro plans currently have no five-hour limit. Its public table does not show fixed 10x or 20x figures for each tier. API-key usage is billed at API rates: subscription multipliers are not API balances, nor should they be conflated with message caps for a model in Chat.

## 02 / Why 10x does not settle everyone's task capacity

The multiplier uses the current Plus allowance as its denominator. `Old 20 × old Plus baseline` and `new 10 × new Plus baseline` need not differ by exactly half if the baseline changes too. Sottiaux said the floor would rise, without publishing enough figures in those posts to calculate each user's new absolute allowance. His separate statement that the new Pro $200 tier has half the old API-dollar equivalent is real, but does not prove that every workflow can complete only half as many tasks.

Model choice, reasoning effort, context length and task shape also affect consumption. Moving suitable work to a cheaper model may increase completed tasks per week; staying with an expensive model may not offset the plan change. Measure comparable tasks on the same model, usage percentages, reset times and any credits purchased on top of the plan instead of comparing tier labels alone.

## 03 / Four useful reactions on X

- [Theo](https://x.com/theo/status/2104825448597479886) welcomed the explicit API-equivalent disclosure while saying the same-price reduction hurts heavy users. This is a value judgment, not evidence that every existing account was reduced immediately.
- [Corey Quinn](https://x.com/quinnypig/status/2104828602902856017) compared the announcement's complexity to an AWS pricing page. Needing to calculate a changing baseline, tier multiplier and credits makes an allowance difficult to verify.
- [Kun Chen](https://x.com/kunchenguid/status/2104830224785605105) acknowledged OpenAI's model efficiency but argued that changing subscription value may give competitors room to reprice. That is a market forecast, not an observed outcome.
- [Gia](https://x.com/giadotai/status/2104834232283766784) asked what happens to existing 20x accounts and how the prior pause in new sign-ups relates to this adjustment. Account notices and the usage dashboard should settle individual transition terms.

These are personal views. Some X posts may not expand without sign-in; the summaries distinguish reactions from subscription terms.

## 04 / Check your own subscription

In ChatGPT, verify whether you are on Plus, Pro $100 or Pro $200, then inspect Work / Codex remaining usage, reset time and the expiry of any bonus credits. Existing subscribers should keep the transition date from their subscription notice and compare **similar tasks on the same model** before and after it. If the dashboard conflicts with the notice, retain a timestamped record without personal details and contact official support; avoid buying multiple accounts to bypass limits.
